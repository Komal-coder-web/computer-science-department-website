About Our Department

The Department of Computer Science at GVM Girls College, Sonipat was established in 1995 with the vision to empower women through quality technical education. Our department has grown to become one of the premier computer science departments in the region.

We offer state-of-the-art facilities including modern computer labs, AI & robotics lab, and a well-stocked digital library. Our faculty comprises highly qualified professionals committed to academic excellence.

With strong industry collaborations and a 100% placement record, we prepare our students for successful careers in technology.
